# RSI 80-20 strategy (TradingStrategyGuides) on four FX pairs (pre-registration)

Date: 2026-10-18. User: "Let's try another RSI trading strategy, explained here:
https://tradingstrategyguides.com/rsi-trading-strategy/ Built it locally as EA and backtest on major forex pairs
(EURUSD, GBPUSD, USDCHF, GBPJPY)". The page (downloaded 2026-10-18, read with its recap card and example charts)
describes the "80-20" strategy; the other RSI systems on the page (RSI 20 trendlines, RSI 4/9 cross, RSI 2) are not
part of it. Rules were read independently by three readers (literal text / example charts / implementation edge
cases) and reconciled (workflow wf_314b2268-afe) before any code was final.

## Rules as coded (`mql5/RSI8020_EA.mq5` v1.00, magic 261020, comment RSI8020, EACore)
RSI(8) on close, levels 20/80 inclusive. Buy side (sells mirror):
1. A: a closed bar whose low is the lowest low of the 50 bars ending at it (ties count) with RSI <= 20. The first such
   bar arms the setup and stays A; a later lower low with lower RSI neither replaces nor cancels it.
2. B (divergence): the first later bar with a low below A's low and RSI above A's RSI (strict, vs A only).
3. A close above A's high before B cancels (invented: the steps come in order); an outside bar that is B and also
   closes above A's high counts as B, and the trigger needs a later bar.
4. Trigger: after B, the first bar closing above A's high; market buy at the next bar's open.
5. Expiry (invented): no trigger within 50 bars of A -> setup dropped.
6. Stop: lowest low from A to the trigger bar - 0.1 x ATR(14) (sells: highest high + 0.1 ATR + spread); target 3R
   from the fill ("at least a 1 to 3 profit vs. risk level"). No other exit.
7. One position per symbol; both sides reset at entry and are not tracked while in a position; both sides triggering
   on one bar -> skip. Spread cap 50 points; a spread-blocked entry is retried for 60 minutes with the stop price
   kept and R recomputed (H4/D1 bars open at the 00:00 rollover).

## Step 0: replay checks (`reports/rsi8020/check.py`)
The state machine is replayed independently in Python from the EA's own bar log (OHLC, RSI, ATR per bar) and must
give the same signals (side, A, stop within 2e-5); trades have 3R targets, stop exits near -1 R, one position at a
time; the 50-bar flags are recomputed. Runs (real ticks): EURUSD H1 2024-2025 (lows/highs), EURUSD H1 2024-2025
(closes), GBPJPY H1 2026-01..10 (wide stop, 3-digit symbol).

## Stage A (`reports/rsi8020/stage_a.py`, Darwinex, 2022-01-01..2026-10-01, 1% risk, 1:30)
Pairs EURUSD, GBPUSD, USDCHF, GBPJPY x timeframes D1 (the article's own recommendation), H4 and M30 (its example
charts), H1. Variants (from the reconciliation; at most two alternatives):
| variant | change |
|---|---|
| `base` | rules above |
| `close` | A and B judged on closes (`InpExtremeMode` 1): "closed the lowest of the last 50" |
| `wide` | stop at least 3 x ATR(14) from the entry (`InpWideStopATR` 3): the only stop the article draws (USDCHF H4) |
Data: the "real ticks" model is used throughout, but Darwinex has real ticks only for EURUSD 2022+, GBPUSD 2024+,
USDCHF and GBPJPY 2026+; elsewhere the tester generates ticks from 1-minute bars (understates spread at entries).
Gate A per variant x timeframe on the 4 pairs pooled (the article: "any currency pair"): trades >= 150, pooled PF
(by R) >= 1.00, net R > 0 in both 2022-24 and 2025-26, and >= 3 of 4 pairs net positive. D1 and H4 are expected to
fall short of 150 trades (EURUSD H1 gave ~15 signals a year in the check), which is a fail by the gate's own rule.
Plateau for passers: expiry 30 / 80 and lookback 40 / 60 (pooled mean PF >= 1.00, >= 3 of 4 profitable), then
prop_mc and the joint rule against the 4-EA Darwinex control, pre-registered separately.

## Expectation
Reversal entries after a 50-bar extreme with RSI divergence and a tight swing stop at 3R need about 27% winners
after costs. Fade systems tested so far (HoLo, FZMR, VWAP_EMA_Cross) showed no bounce after the signal. Expected pooled
PF 0.7-1.0, 0 passes; the wide stop raises the win rate but not the edge.
