# MACD_Cross_EA on M15 and M30 signal bars (pre-registration)

Date: 2026-10-19. User: "let's test it out on 15m, 30m timeframes", after a GUI run on an M15 chart turned out to be the
H1 strategy (the EA trades its `InpTimeframe` input, 16385 = H1, whatever the chart; all 378 entries matched our H1
Darwinex run). Server: Darwinex-Demo (user's choice).

## Runs (`reports/macd_tf/macd_tf.py`, Darwinex-Demo, real ticks, 2022-01-01..2026-10-01, 1% risk)
MACD_Cross_EA v1.46 defaults (MACD 16/26/9, H4 EMA200 trend filter, SL 0.5% / TP 3.75% of price, break-even at +0.5%,
no trailing, up to 3 same-side positions, spread cap 150, daily -4% / monthly -12% stops), changing only `InpTimeframe`:
| cell | InpTimeframe | tester period |
|---|---|---|
| `h1` (control) | H1 | H1 |
| `m30` | M30 | M30 |
| `m15` | M15 | M15 |
Stops and targets are % of price, so they do not shrink with the bar: on M15 a 0.5% stop spans several bar ranges.
The `h1` run also serves as MACD's Darwinex parity check for v1.46 (entries vs `v145_default_20261011_092414`, reported only).

## Checks
Gate A per cell: trades >= 150, PF >= 1.00, net R > 0 in both 2022-24 and 2025-26.
Prop: standalone prop_mc per cell (--bt-risk 1.0, --risk 0.3,0.5, 10k sims, seeds 1-3), P(pass both phases).

## Hypothesis
MACD crosses on M15/M30 come 2-4 times as often as on H1 and are noisier, with the same % stop. Expected: more trades,
lower PF (M30 about 1.2-1.4, M15 about 1.0-1.2), at least one regime weaker than H1; standalone pass rate below H1.

## Decision rule
An M15/M30 cell becomes a candidate only if it passes Gate A and its standalone P(pass both) at 0.3% is >= the h1 cell's
on all 3 seeds. A candidate then gets a plateau test (MACD fast 12/20, SL 0.4/0.6%) and the joint 4-EA test as a
replacement for H1 MACD, pre-registered separately. Otherwise the timeframes are closed and H1 stays. No retuning in this
round.

**No candidate.** Both lower timeframes pass Gate A, but each loses about 18-25 points of standalone pass rate against
H1 at 0.3%, and its p95 drawdown is 1.6-2 times larger. The hypothesis held on direction; M30 and M15 PFs came in at
1.23-1.24. With the same % stop, 1.6-2.1 times as many crosses earn a half to two fifths of the R per trade. M15 made only
+28.5 R in 2022-24, and its total comes mostly from the 2025-26 trend. The timeframes are closed and H1 stays.

The h1 run doubles as MACD v1.46's Darwinex parity check: all 955 entries are identical to the v1.45 control
(`v145_default_20261011_092414`), with 0 new-only and 0 control-only.
