# EP001 15-minute opening range (TradingView port) on EURUSD, GBPUSD and XAUUSD M5 at the Asia, London and New York opens: pre-registration (2026-11-07)

**Request:** "implement this pinescript into a mq5 ea and backtest it on EURUSD, GBPUSD, XAUUSD on M5 for Asia, London and NY sessions openings" (link to the TradingView script "EP001 - 15m Opening Range Audit (Fade vs Breakout)", then the full Pine v6 source pasted by the user on 2026-11-07).

**Source.** Open-source Pine v6 strategy, `tradingview.com/script/bTnJjVXu`. The pasted code carries no licence text; TradingView's house rules set a default licence for open-source scripts, which the user should confirm on the script page before any live use (the deploy rule in `docs/gates.md` asks for "no third-party licence or approval needed"). The screen is research evidence only. The port is `mql5/EP001_ORB_EA.mq5` (EACore base, magic 291107, compiles with 0 errors and 0 warnings).

**The script, from its source.**
- **Range:** the high, low, open and close of the first 15 minutes of the New York day (`0930-0945`, three M5 candles).
- **Filter:** range height >= 0.25 x the daily ATR(14) (`request.security(..., "D", ta.atr(14)[1])`), tested on the first candle after the range. At most one trade a day (`done_today`).
- **Signal candle** (closed M5 candle, inside the trade window, after the range): hammer `green, lower wick > 2 x body, upper wick < lower wick / 3` or red engulfing-green; mirrored for the red inverted hammer and the green engulfing-red.
- **Breakout mode** (the script's default, "Flipped: Momentum Breakout"): window `0930-1130`. Long: the candle closes above the range high and is a green hammer or a green candle engulfing a red one. Short: closes below the range low and is a red inverted hammer or a red candle engulfing a green one. Stop at the candle's low / high (hammer) or 2 ticks beyond it (engulfing); target `close + 2 x (close - stop)`, both as fixed prices from the signal candle; entry at the next candle's open.
- **Fade mode** ("Original: Mean-Reversion Fade"): window `0930-1100`. Short: the range candle closed up (`or_close > or_open`), the signal candle's high is above the range high and it is a red inverted hammer or red engulfing; limit sell at the signal close (hammer) or the previous candle's close (engulfing); stop at the high (+2 ticks for an engulfing); target the range low. Long mirrored with a range that closed down.
- **Sizing in the script:** 10% of equity per entry, 0.04% commission, 1 tick slippage. Not used here (below).
- **Author's claims (TradingView page, not checked here):** fade lost money (11-20% wins) on NVDA, PLTR and BTCUSDT over one year; breakout at 2R won 33-35% on equities. A 2R target breaks even at 33.3% before costs.

**The port (what is identical, what is not).** Identical: the range, the 25% ATR filter, the patterns, the stop and target formulas, one signal a day, the 120 / 90 minute windows, signals read on closed M5 candles. Differences, all fixed here:

| # | Pine | EA | Why |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | New York 09:30 only | `InpSession`: New York 09:30, London 08:00 (Europe/London), Asia 09:00 (Asia/Tokyo) | the request. Tokyo 09:00 = 00:00 UTC |
| 2 | `America/New_York` clock | Darwinex server time = New York + 7 h, with the US and EU daylight-saving rules in the EA (no `TimeGMT()`, which the tester gets wrong) | see the clock table |
| 3 | `ta.atr(14)[1]` of the daily series | Wilder ATR (RMA, seeded with the mean of the first 14 true ranges) over 150 completed Darwinex D1 candles, ending with the last completed day | read as the script's own wording, "confirmed prior-day data" |
| 4 | `pyramiding = 0`: an opposite signal reverses an open position | a signal found while a position is open is skipped and counted | simpler; the count is reported |
| 5 | an unfilled fade limit order stays until it fills, days later | it lapses at the end of the window (`ORDER_TIME_SPECIFIED`); a limit already reached at the next open fills at the market, as Pine fills at the open | the Pine behaviour is unintended |
| 6 | 10% of equity notional, 0.04% commission, 1 tick slippage | `InpRiskValue` 0.5% of balance per trade, the lot follows the stop distance; the tester's real ticks, spreads and the symbol's commission | the earlier ORB studies used 0.5% (`2026-10-26-orb-ea-assets-prereg.md`); R is comparable |
| 7 | `syminfo.mintick` | `SYMBOL_TRADE_TICK_SIZE`: 0.00001 on EURUSD and GBPUSD (2 ticks = 0.2 pip), 0.01 on XAUUSD | |
| 8 | day resets at New York midnight | day = the server date; each session open and window lie inside one server date | |
| 9 | candles from the TradingView feed | Darwinex bid candles; a long enters at the ask | real costs |

**Cells.** 3 symbols x 3 sessions x 2 modes = 18. The nine **breakout** cells (the script's default) are the question. The nine **fade** cells are the script's other mode, run for comparison on the same rules. Keys in the runner: `ep_<E|G|X>_<N|L|A>` (breakout), `epf_...` (fade); symbols EURUSD, GBPUSD, XAUUSD; sessions New York, London, Asia.

**Question.** Does the script's opening-range candle-pattern rule, at any of the three session opens on EURUSD, GBPUSD or XAUUSD, pass Gates A, B and C as an add-on candidate?

**Setup.** Darwinex-Demo, M5, real ticks, $10,000, 1:100, the EA's defaults except `InpSession` and `InpMode` per cell (the profile in `scripts/ea_profiles.py`): risk 0.5%, max spread 150 points, no spread wait, daily loss stop 4%, monthly 12%, account throttle off. The report's input list must show the cell's session and mode. **Not stateful:** every trade has a stop and a target and only one position is open at a time, so Gate B is the 2022-23 run joined with the Gate A run.

**Decision rule (fixed before any gate run).**
- Gates A, B and C as in `docs/gates.md`. A: trades >= 40, PF >= 1.0, net > 0, no stop-out. B: trades >= 150 (the weekly-rule floor of 80 does not apply: the EA can trade every day), PF >= 1.0, net > 0 in 2022-24 and in 2025-26. C is the add-on version: net > 0 and PF >= 1.0, inconclusive below 90% real-tick history. A cell that cannot reach 150 trades fails Gate B on that count; the floor is not lowered after seeing the counts.
- **No two-rejects stop.** The user named all nine breakout cells; each is run to its verdict. The fade cells follow the same gates.
- The runner's speed and log guards apply (two-week probe over 900 s = too slow, over 100 MB of agent log = the strip-logging rule). The 150-trade early reject cannot trigger (at most 10 trades in two weeks).
- A passing cell is "add-on test due", which needs its own spec.
- **Loss-limiting variant (named now):** only for a cell that passes A to C and fails an account profile: the account throttle on (`InpThrottleDDPct` 3.0, factor 0.5), an input the EA already has. Default and variant are reported side by side; no sweep.

**Reported for every run** (net USD always with its sizing): trades, net USD at 0.5% risk, total R and mean R (stop from the Orders table), PF, tester equity drawdown, win rate, net by year, longest hold, positions held more than 30 days and positions closed only by the test end (a **STUCK POSITIONS** note if any), the largest lot, and the EA's own end-of-run line (days with a range, days passing the ATR filter, signals, signals skipped because a position was open or the price was already past the stop). Stops and targets only, no time exit: a position opened on Friday can sit over the weekend.

**Data check.** The three sessions of a symbol run on the same candles, so their M5 bar counts must agree within 1%; a window more than 8% under weekdays x 288 M5 bars (EURUSD, GBPUSD) is inconclusive. Gold's daily break is allowed for by comparing it only with its own sessions.

**Expectation (written before the runs).** The earlier five-stage ORB (`2026-10-26-orb-ea-assets-prereg.md`) lost in 18 of 18 cells at 0.5% risk, PF 0.55-0.95, with the same sessions on the same symbols, and the older base ORB lost about its cost on EURUSD and GBPUSD. This script differs by an ATR filter and a one-candle pattern trigger, which cut the trades sharply, so the count may be the first obstacle (Gate A needs 40, Gate B 150). M5 stops are a few pips (gold a few dollars) against spreads of 0.1-0.3 pips, which is 3-10% of R on forex. Expect most cells to fail; gold is the likeliest to pass Gate A (it rose from about 1,990 to 5,430 inside 2024-26, which flatters any breakout rule), so Gates B and C decide.

**Known limits.**
- **Tester vs TradingView:** the candles, spreads and ATR differ from the script's chart; no reference run of the Pine script exists here, so the port is checked against the rules, not against the author's trade list.
- **ATR timing:** Pine's `request.security` on historical intraday bars may lag the daily value by one more day than the live value; the EA uses the last completed day, as the script's wording says.
- **Fade limits** are cancelled at the window's end (difference 5).
- **Asia** is Tokyo 09:00; Sydney and other opens are not tested.

**Port check (before this spec; not part of any gate; `reports/oct25/ep001_probe.py`, log `reports/oct25/ep001_probe.log`).** Nine real-tick runs of 4-6 weeks around the daylight-saving switches (2024-03-01..04-15 holds the US switch of 10 March and the EU switch of 31 March; 2024-10-15..11-15 holds the EU end of 27 October and the US end of 3 November): EURUSD breakout in all three sessions and both windows, EURUSD New York fade (March window), XAUUSD New York breakout (March) and Asia (October).
- **Clock:** 246 range days logged; every session open the EA used equals the open worked out from the time-zone database (`zoneinfo`) and server = New York + 7 h. 0 mismatches, including London's 11:00 days in the off-weeks. The range was built from three M5 candles on every day.
- **Formulas:** 9 signals logged. For each, the pattern, the close outside the range, the stop, the target and (fade) the limit price were re-derived from the Pine formulas: 9 of 9 consistent.
- **Speed and log:** 14-16 s a run (about 16 s of that is tester start-up), agent log growth 0-1 MB: no strip needed.
- **ATR:** EURUSD 51-80 pips, XAUUSD $19-40, in line with real daily ranges.
- **Days passing the ATR filter** (the first obstacle): EURUSD New York 2 of 31 and 4 of 23, London 1 of 31 and 3 of 23, Asia 0 of 31 and 1 of 23; XAUUSD New York 12 of 30, Asia 0 of 23.
- **Trades (no verdict):** 9 trades, net +218.60 USD at 0.5% risk (about +4.4 R at $50 a R); by run: EURUSD New York breakout +96.88, London -56.38 and +23.93, Asia +96.45, New York fade -69.37, XAUUSD New York +127.09. One trade shows what a tiny stop does: EURUSD London 2024-10-30, an engulfing candle's stop 2.5 pips from the entry, 1.99 lots, stopped out in 4 seconds for -60.53 USD (the spread is a visible share of a 2.5-pip R).
- **Trade rate:** at these rates EURUSD makes about 8 trades a year per session (Gate A's 40 over 2.75 years is out of reach), gold New York about 25 a year (about 70 in Gate A, about 120 in Gate B against a floor of 150). The standard floors will decide most cells on the trade count; see the floors question below.
